Experiment 23 · Regularised networks

Do the networks improve with a lower learning rate and strong regularisation?

Purpose: in Experiment 22 the MLP and the MLP + GRU did best after a single epoch, then overfit the training seasons. This experiment retrains both with a ten times lower learning rate, more dropout and strong weight decay, to see whether slower, more constrained training gets more out of the same inputs.

1 Fire Centres · train 2018–2021 · thresholds 2022 · evaluate 2023 · 2024 excluded

Setup

  • Only the training settings change. Label, training draw, inputs, input scaling, layer sizes, thresholds and scoring are Experiment 22's. Experiment 21's CatBoost and Experiment 22's MLP are loaded, not retrained, and reproduce their earlier numbers exactly.
  • One seed per model, as in Experiment 22. The epoch is still chosen on held-out weeks of the training seasons (one 7-day block in six), never on 2022 or 2023.
