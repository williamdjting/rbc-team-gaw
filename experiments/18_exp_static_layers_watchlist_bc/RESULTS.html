Experiment 18 · Static layers, quiet-cell watch list, wildfire flag

Do land, fuel and industry layers help, and can quiet cells get their own product?

Purpose: Experiments 16–17 showed that fires in quiet cells never reach the forecast's alert threshold, and that cells burning with no BC Wildfire Service record sit on flat, low farmland and oil and gas sites. This experiment adds static layers to the forecast, ranks quiet cells for a separate watch list, and builds a flag that separates wildfires from other burning.

1 Fire Centres · train 2018–2021 · thresholds 2022 · evaluate 2023 · 2024 excluded

Setup

  • Static layers (17 inputs): elevation and slope; fuel-type shares; Agricultural Land Reserve share; oil and gas facilities and wells in the cell and its neighbours; cutblock area harvested in the 3 previous years. Facilities, wells and cutblocks count only if dated before the season.
  • A. Next-day forecast: CatBoost with Experiment 13's features, with and without the static layers, against "fire in this cell yesterday". Thresholds chosen on 2022, applied to 2023. Intervals resample whole weeks of 2023.
  • B. Quiet-cell watch list: a quiet cell has no detection in it or its neighbours in the past 7 days. Each day, each Fire Centre lists its N highest-ranked quiet cells. Rankings: both forecast models, a CatBoost trained only on quiet cells (adding Experiment 17's history), and two simple rankings.
  • C. Wildfire flag: for each detected cell-day, the chance it is not linked to a BCWS wildfire (Experiment 17's link to final perimeters and ignition points). Inputs are the static layers, the detection itself (intensity, night share, non-vegetation type, fire-complex size, recent activity), earlier seasons and location; no BCWS inputs.

Conclusion

  • Forecast: static layers changed BC-wide F1 (one-cell margin) by +0.003 over Experiment 13's model (95% interval -0.002 to +0.012); against the rule, +0.008 (-0.005 to +0.022). CONCLUSION_FORECAST
  • Watch list: with 50 quiet cells per Fire Centre per day, the best ranking (CatBoost, Experiment 13 features) had 27% of 2023 quiet fires on its list within the 7 days before they started, against 0.3% for a random list on the day before. CONCLUSION_WATCH
  • Wildfire flag: in 2022, F1 0.82 against 0.65 for the simple rule; in 2023, 0.30 against 0.07. CONCLUSION_FLAG
  • Next: CONCLUSION_NEXT
  • Reproduction check against Experiment 17 was not run (subset of Fire Centres).
  • Terrain, fuels and the Agricultural Land Reserve are current layers, applied to every year; they change slowly, but fuels can change after a large fire.
  • The wildfire link depends on BCWS records and final perimeters. Large perimeters (2023) take in nearby non-wildfire burning, so labels are least reliable in big fire years.
  • Untuned CatBoost, one seed. 2024 remains excluded.
