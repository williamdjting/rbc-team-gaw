Experiment 35 · Fire starts with networks

Do an MLP and an MLP + GRU predict fire starts better than CatBoost?

Purpose: Experiment 34 trained CatBoost on fire starts only, the first detection in a cell after a quiet week. This experiment trains Experiment 23's regularised MLP and MLP + 14-day GRU on the same fire starts, to see whether a network, or a model that sees the past two weeks day by day, ranks the next fire start better.

1 Fire Centres · train 2018–2021 · thresholds 2022 · evaluate 2023 · 2024 excluded

Setup

  • Fire start: a cell positive on the label on day t (S-NPP or NOAA-20 from 2021, S-NPP before, one-day gaps filled) with no detection from the same source in the cell or its six neighbours on days t-7 to t-1. Only these quiet cell-days can start a fire, so every forecast is trained and scored on quiet cell-days only.
  • Networks: Experiment 23's MLP (256-256-128) and MLP + 64-unit GRU over the past 14 days, with its regularised settings (learning rate 1e-4, dropout 0.3, weight decay 0.1, early stopping on held-out weeks). Trained on Experiment 34's rows: quiet cell-days of 2018–2021, all fire starts plus Experiment 21's 1% draw of the rest, with Experiment 21's inputs. Input scaling is refit on these rows; the output starts at the training base rate.
  • References, loaded unchanged: Experiment 34's fire-start CatBoost, Experiment 21's next-day CatBoost, Experiment 22's next-day MLP, two simple rankings and random lists. Experiment 34's numbers reproduce exactly.
  • Two ways to judge: F1 with a threshold tuned on 2022, as before; and a daily watch list of the top N quiet cells per Fire Centre, which suits rare events better than F1.

Conclusion

  • Training uses S-NPP-only fire starts in 2018–2020; 2021–2023 also use NOAA-20, which finds more starts.
  • About half of quiet onsets have no BCWS record (Experiment 16): many are likely agricultural, slash or industrial burns, not wildfires.
  • 2023 has been used to compare many experiments; only 2024 will give an untouched number.
