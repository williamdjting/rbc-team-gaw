The forecast · same grid, two distinct experiment tracks

Tomorrow’s hotspot activity, one H3 cell at a time

Input

Past satellite observations and available environmental history, ending on UTC day t−1.

Output

A score and thresholded alert for each of 234,554 H3 resolution-7 cell entries across six Fire Centres.

Target

A detection on day t, or a one-day missing observation bracketed by detections. This is hotspot activity, not verified ignition.

y(c,t) = D(c,t) OR [D(c,t−1) AND D(c,t+1)]
TrackWhat D meansEvaluation
21–22S-NPP OR NOAA-20 detection2023; exact-cell and spatial-margin scores
25–26S-NPP detection only2023 and 2024; exact-cell scores only

Gap filling is for retrospective labels only, never input features. A zero label is not confirmed fire absence. H3 resolution 7 is approximately 5 km² per cell; lightning is coarser, on a 0.1° grid.

Margin improvement

The no-calendar model reaches 0.824, versus 0.810 for the rule. Difference ≈ +0.015; 95% interval [+0.005, +0.025].

Operating point matters

At the rule’s daily alert count, margin F1 is 0.807. The headline gain depends on the selected threshold and number of alerts, not just ranking.

All numbers are BC-wide pooled F1. Thresholds were selected on 2022 separately by model, centre and scoring definition. Exact-cell and margin F1 are different tasks; F1 is not accuracy.

24 · frozen two-satellite MLP

We preserved the saved checkpoint, scaler and 2022 thresholds. Matching NOAA-20 and other 2024 inputs were unavailable, so this model was not tested on 2024.

Decision

Proceed with the sources available: a new S-NPP + lightning model. Keep the two-satellite model unchanged. This changes both the feature set and the label, so the new result cannot be compared directly with 0.626 from Experiment 22.

Keep every cell-day. Do not evaluate on a sampled set of quiet negatives. Save the joined features, labels and predictions for inspection.

The persistence baseline uses only yesterday’s S-NPP detection. It has no lightning. That comparison tests whether the complete MLP beats the simple rule, not lightning’s contribution alone.

What changes

The MLP produces 1,886 fewer false positives than persistence, but also 959 fewer true positives. Higher precision comes at lower recall.

Uncertainty

MLP minus persistence: −0.0084 F1, paired 95% interval [−0.0184, +0.0023]. No demonstrated overall F1 improvement.

This test uses the S-NPP-only label. Do not interpret the difference from Experiment 22’s 0.626 as a measured decline of the same model on the same target. AP is median per-centre AP, not pooled AP.

Why the provincial score matters

The MLP leads in Cariboo, Kamloops and Northwest, and trails in Coastal, Prince George and Southeast. Pooled F1 combines confusion counts; it is not the average of the six F1 values.

Prince George accounts for 15,299 of 20,082 positive 2024 cell-days in this grid. Its performance has a large effect on the pooled result. “Positive cell-days” does not mean distinct fires.

Matched ablation · use the year selector

Lightning improves ranking; the F1 effect is small

With vs without lightning

Read the metrics separately

F1 measures the selected yes/no alerts. Average precision measures ranking without one selected threshold. Median regional AP rises in both years, while the F1 change reverses sign.

2023: F1 0.5833 → 0.5813; median AP 0.5790 → 0.5910. 2024: F1 0.5547 → 0.5586; median AP 0.4674 → 0.4765. These are observed differences from one matched seed.

Conclusion

The 2024 gain is +0.385 F1 percentage points (+0.69% relative), but it is not clearly distinguishable from zero. We do not yet have a consistent, clearly established F1 benefit.

2,000 paired resamples of whole ISO weeks. Intervals describe within-year variation, not training-seed or future-season uncertainty. One F1 point = 0.01 F1, not one point of accuracy.

Week 4 conclusion

The baseline remains hard to beat

Supported by this week’s results

21–22: richer inputs and an MLP narrow the gap on the two-satellite target.
25: the S-NPP/lightning MLP does not clearly beat persistence in 2024.
26: lightning improves observed median AP, but its F1 contribution is small and uncertain.

What we cannot claim

No proof that lightning has no value. No completed BC-wide regularisation result. No 2024 result for the frozen two-satellite MLP. No evidence that these hotspot models reliably predict verified ignitions.

Next · development onlyCheck robustnessRepeat matched seeds and temporal folds within development years.
Next · operating objectiveChoose alert costsSet a recall or alert-budget objective before a new evaluation.
Next · dataRestore richer inputsObtain matching NOAA-20/weather and a fresh evaluation season before renewed final-test claims.
