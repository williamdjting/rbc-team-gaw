Conclusion

  • The definition sets the ceiling. Median best-model F1 moves from 0.538 on the exact cell to 0.761 with a one-cell margin, with the same models and data. Definitions with a median of at least 0.70: Res-7, one-cell margin, Res-6, one-cell margin.
  • The one-cell margin keeps 5 km² cells. It accepts a forecast that is off by one cell (about 2–3 km), which is how fire spread and the satellite's pixel position move detections. This is the usual neighbourhood check in forecast verification.
  • Exact-cell F1 should stay in every report next to the chosen definition, so results remain comparable with Experiments 02–07 and 13.
  • Next: NOAA-20 detections reduce missed days under every definition; ERA5-Land day-t rain and wind target the fires that stop.
  • Targets are recorded detections, not ignitions or confirmed absence. Coverage remains unknown.
  • Margin scoring: a flagged cell is correct if a detection (any Fire Centre) falls in it or a neighbour; a detected cell is caught if it or an in-centre neighbour is flagged. Res-6 scores are the highest res-7 score among each cell's children.
  • Thresholds are chosen on 2022 per centre, definition and model. Untuned models and a single seed; differences under about 0.01 F1 are within noise. 2024 remains excluded.
