Understanding the labels

What "fire" and "no fire" really mean

FactWhat it means for us
Satellite files only list firesEvery "no fire" (99.8% of rows) is filled in by us
"No fire" = nothing seenClouds or smoke can hide a fire
Some heat isn't wildfireGas flares, mills, farm and slash burns
BCWS = official fire recordsConfirms which heat is a real wildfire

Heat with no BCWS record

  • Not all industrial: also missed or late-logged real fires.
  • In big fire years, only ~3% of heat is unexplained.
  • But for new, isolated heat it's ~45%.
  • So cleaning labels matters for new fires, not overall.
