Experiment 33 · Final test on 2024

How do the forecasts do on a season none of them has seen?

Purpose: 2023 was used to compare many models, so its scores are optimistic. 2024 was never loaded until now. The saved models of Experiments 20–23 are scored on it once, with thresholds chosen on 2022, nothing retrained or tuned.

1 Fire Centres · trained on 2018–2021 · thresholds 2022 · tested on 2024 · models and metrics fixed in README.md before scoring

Setup

  • Label: S-NPP (FIRMS) or NOAA-20 (CWFIS) VIIRS detection in the cell on the target day, one-day gaps filled, as in 2023. NOAA-21, new in 2024, is not used.
  • Inputs for 2024: downloaded and built into separate files with the same scripts as 2018–2023. Rebuilding 2023's feature tables with the 2024 script reproduces Experiment 02's exactly.
  • Checks: on 2022, every forecast reproduces Experiments 21 and 22 on every Fire Centre, so the thresholds are the ones used before.
  • A quieter season: 2024 had far fewer fire cell-days than 2023, but similar to 2022 in most centres.
